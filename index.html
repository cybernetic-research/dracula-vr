Bring your own Dracula Unleashed disc (see README). Desktop: click the desk. VR: point and pull the trigger.

Looking for the game media…

Make the zips on your computer from the media folder prepared from your own disc (tools/pack-media.sh: several zips of about 1 GB), copy them to this device (on a Quest: its Download folder, over USB), then choose them here, together or one at a time. They are copied into this browser's storage and the game then runs with no computer and no network. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
